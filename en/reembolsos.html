Oolia Refund Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This translation is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version prevails.

Oolia is free. The only things you pay for are premium (a one-time payment that unlocks the themes) and tips (voluntary contributions). Both are paid through Google Play, so refunds also go through Google Play: we never receive your payment details and cannot charge you anything outside Play.

Within the first 48 hours

You can request the refund yourself from Google Play: on play.google.com, My account, Order history, choose the purchase and tap “Request a refund”. Google decides according to its refund policy.

After 48 hours

Write to us at ooliasupport@gmail.com with the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and tell us what happened. We review it and, if appropriate, refund you through Google Play.

  • Premium: if premium doesn’t activate or the themes don’t work and we can’t fix it, we refund the payment. Once refunded, premium is no longer active.
  • Tips: they are voluntary contributions and don’t unlock anything. If you gave one by mistake, write to us and we will refund it.
  • Purchases made without permission (for example, by a child using your phone): write to us as soon as possible and we will help you with Google Play.

What isn’t paid for

The mini-game’s shells and accessories cannot be bought with money, so there is nothing to refund for them.

Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits the rights consumer law gives you.
